How to read this tracker
Momentum tab — your daily baseline
  • Sort by 1H to see where money is flowing right now
  • Green bars = sectors gaining strength this hour
  • Confirm with 1D and 1M — single-timeframe moves are noise
  • Buy signal = leading on all three timeframes at once
  • Reduce signal = lagging on all three — avoid new longs
Hourly flow — intraday timing
  • Steeper slope = more institutional urgency behind the move
  • Sector surging but your stock is flat = expect a catch-up
  • Stock rallying but sector is flat = likely to reverse. Trust sector.
  • Wait for sector to confirm before entering a stock position
  • If the leader at 9:30am is still leading at 3:30pm — that's the real story
Rotation events — the early warning
  • Rotation event = a sector just displaced another in the top 3
  • Risk-on rotation (Tech/Fins) → add aggressive longs
  • Defensive rotation (Utes/Staples rising) → tighten stops now
  • 3+ defensive events in one session = reduce overall exposure
  • Cluster of events in 1–2 hours = algorithmic, high conviction
Weekly log — confirm the regime
  • 3 consecutive days same sector leading = confirmed trend
  • Erratic daily leaders = choppy, low-conviction — reduce size
  • Weekly signal flips risk-on to defensive = reassess all positions
  • Buy Monday when prior Friday showed strong breadth (>65%)
  • Same sector appearing in both leading and lagging across weeks = transition forming

How institutions move money — and why
Pension funds and insurance companies
Move slowly and in size — look for 3–5 day sustained sector leadership, not single-day spikes. They rotate into XLU, XLV, XLRE as rates stabilize (yield substitute). When you see these defensives leading for multiple consecutive days despite a weak broad market, pension money is at work. Key trigger: quarterly rebalancing at end of March, June, Sept, and Dec. Watch for outsized volume in defensive ETFs those weeks. This is mechanical, not macro-driven.
Hedge funds — macro and quant
Fast and directional. Responsible for the sudden rotation events you see in the hourly flow tab. When XLK and XLC surge simultaneously in the first hour with no news, quant momentum models are loading. When XLE spikes alone with no broad market support, macro funds are hedging inflation or geopolitical risk. Key tell: rotation events that cluster in a 1–2 hour window are algorithmic, not retail. Multiple simultaneous rotations = high conviction institutional shift. Follow it, don't fade it.
Mutual funds — active managers
Benchmark-driven and forced to own the leading sector to avoid tracking error. If XLK leads for 2+ weeks, active managers are compelled to overweight tech or risk underperforming their benchmark. This self-reinforcing momentum is visible in the weekly log — use it to identify 2+ week leadership trends. They distribute into strength near highs (which is why rotation events spike near market tops). The weekly log is your best tool for spotting this: consistent leadership that suddenly gets erratic = distribution underway.
Market makers and options dealers
Not directional traders but their delta-hedging amplifies moves. When breadth rises above 70%, dealers are hedging calls by buying stock — creating self-reinforcing rallies. When breadth collapses below 40%, dealer gamma flips negative and they sell into weakness — accelerating selloffs. The breadth metric on your tracker is the best proxy for dealer-flow. Breadth expansion = tail wind. Breadth collapse = head wind. Watch it diverge from price: price up + breadth falling = distribution, not accumulation.
Corporate buybacks
Steady, predictable, and concentrated in XLK and XLC (tech dominates buybacks by dollar volume). Peak window: 2 weeks after earnings through end of quarter. This is why tech holds up in mild pullbacks — corporate treasuries are consistent buyers. Buyback blackout period (2 weeks before earnings) removes this support. Watch for XLK vulnerability during blackout windows: the same selling pressure that was absorbed by buybacks now hits the open market. Use the weekly log to spot XLK underperformance during blackout weeks as an early warning.
When institutions rotate — the calendar
Daily session patterns
  • 9:30–10:30am — opening rotation: real institutional intent shows here first
  • 11am–1pm — lunchtime drift: low conviction, fade strong morning moves carefully
  • 2–3pm — macro headlines window: Fed speakers, data revisions often hit here
  • 3–4pm — power hour: rebalancing into close drives final sector direction
  • Leader at open still leading at 3:30pm = high-conviction, follow it
Weekly session patterns
  • Monday: continuation of Friday close; low volume — watch, don't trade size
  • Tuesday–Wednesday: highest institutional conviction; best days for new entries
  • Thursday: options flow distorts sector moves (theta decay near expiry)
  • Friday: risk reduction — defensives often drift up regardless of news
  • Opex Friday (3rd Friday): max volatility before 11am; wait before reading sectors
Monthly and quarterly patterns
  • Month-end window dressing: laggards get sold, winners bought — fade the move
  • Quarter-end pension rebalancing distorts flows for 3–5 days
  • Earnings season start: XLK and XLC get noisy — reduce size until clarity
  • Fed meeting weeks: XLU and XLRE react violently to rate signals
  • Jobs report Friday: XLF leads if strong; XLU leads if weak
Macro trigger quick reference
  • CPI hot → XLE, XLB, XLP lead; XLK, XLRE lag (rates rise)
  • CPI cool → XLK, XLC, XLRE lead; XLE, XLB lag (rates fall)
  • Strong jobs → XLF, XLY, XLI lead (growth confidence)
  • Weak jobs → XLU, XLV, XLP lead (rate-cut hope)
  • Geopolitical event → XLE spikes, XLK sells; check within 30 min for reversal

3-step check before every trade
Step 1 — Is the sector aligned?
  • Check Momentum tab. Is your stock's sector showing Buy or Hold?
  • Sector is Reduce and you want to buy the stock? Don't. Wait.
  • Ideal: sector in top 3 on 1H, 1D, and 1M simultaneously
  • Minimum: sector is neutral but not actively lagging
  • Your stock can only be as strong as its sector allows
Step 2 — Is the broad environment right?
  • Check the environment banner at the top of this tab
  • Bull run → full size. Late cycle → half size. Bear → skip or tiny.
  • Breadth above 55% = green light for longs
  • Breadth below 40% = only defensive positions or cash
  • Environment contradicts trade direction = cut size in half minimum
Step 3 — Is institutional flow aligned?
  • Check Rotation events — are institutions flowing INTO your sector?
  • Sector just rotated OUT of top 3? Wait 1–2 hours before entering
  • 2+ rotation events into your sector today = high conviction signal
  • All 3 steps green = full size entry
  • 2 of 3 = half size. 1 of 3 = paper trade only or skip
